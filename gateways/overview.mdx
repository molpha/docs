The gateway is Molpha's HTTP entry point. It authenticates the caller, checks the subscription or the x402 payment, stamps the round's timestamp, dispatches the round to the selected nodes and returns the signed attestation. Gateways are operated by Molpha and registered on-chain by the protocol authority.

Most integrations use the gateway through the [SDK](/sdk/gateway-api) or [MCP](/mcp/overview). Use the HTTP API directly when you work in another language or need full control.

## Base URL

| Network | URL |
| --- | --- |
| Devnet | `https://gateway.molpha.io` |

## Requests

A round request names a source by its `apiConfig`, a quorum (`signaturesRequired`) and the current `registryVersion`. You never send a `sourceId` or a timestamp:

- The gateway, SDK and nodes each derive the `sourceId` from the `apiConfig`. See [Source ID](/concepts/feeds#source-id).
- The gateway stamps the round on a 100 ms grid after admitting it. See [Round timing](/concepts/attestations#round-timing).
- `registryVersion` must equal the current on-chain version; a stale one gets `400`.

## Authentication

### RequestAuth

`POST /v1/round/execute` carries `subscriptionOwner`, `consumerAuthority` (the owner itself or one of its delegates), `authTimestamp` and `authSig`, an Ed25519 signature over:

```text
keccak256("MOLPHA_REQAUTH_V1" || programId || gatewayPda || sourceId || u8(signaturesRequired) || u64_le(authTimestamp))
```

`authTimestamp` is unix **seconds**, and the gateway rejects it with `401` when it is further from its clock than `requestAuthWindowSeconds` (see `/v1/info`; 60 on devnet). Sign a fresh one for every request. RequestAuth only authorizes use of the subscription: it is not part of the round, the signed message or any payment.

### Sign-in sessions

Instead of RequestAuth, a caller can sign in once and send `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. Sessions are available when [`GET /v1/info`](/gateways/api/info) reports `sessionAuth: true`.

1. `GET /v1/session/challenge?address=<wallet>[&owner=<owner>]` returns the Sign-In-With-X message (`data.message`) and its terms (`data.info`).
2. Sign the message's UTF-8 bytes with the wallet (Ed25519).
3. `POST /v1/session` with a `SIGN-IN-WITH-X` header: base64 JSON of the `info` fields plus `address`, `chainId`, `type: "ed25519"`, `signatureScheme: "siws"` and the base58 `signature`. It answers `201` with `data.token` and `data.expiresAt`.
4. Send `Authorization: Bearer <token>` on `POST /v1/round/execute`, with no `authSig` or `authTimestamp`.
5. `DELETE /v1/session` signs out.

A token is valid only at the gateway that issued it, for 30 minutes by default and never past the subscription term. It identifies the caller only: the subscription and delegate are checked against chain state (cached for a few seconds) on every round. The [hosted MCP server](/mcp/hosted#sign-in-with-your-wallet-siwx) wraps this flow.

### x402

`POST /v1/x402/execute` takes no RequestAuth: the payment is the authorization. See [x402 pay-per-request](/access-models/x402).

## Admission

- **Subscription rounds** need an active subscription, a quorum within the plan's `max_signers`, and remaining quota. Quota is counted per 30-day term across the owner and its delegates, and a delegate's rounds also count against its own `max_data_requests`. A round that fails before completing gives its unit back.
- **x402 rounds** need a verified payment of the quoted price to the protocol treasury.
- The Solana program does not settle rounds individually; the gateway enforces payment and quota at admission.

## Response

Both execute endpoints return `200` with `data.attestation`:

- `payload`: `value`, `sourceId`, `registryVersion`, `signaturesRequired`, `timestamp` (unix ms)
- `signature`: `signature`, `commitment`, `signersBitmap`

plus a decimal `value`, `fresh`, and, in tolerance mode, `aggregation`. The x402 endpoint also returns a `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` header. Field meanings are in [Rounds and attestations](/concepts/attestations).

<Warning>
A gateway response is not proof of anything on its own. Verify the attestation before you act on it: see the [consumer checklist](/verifiers/overview#consumer-checklist).
</Warning>

## Status codes

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `400` | Invalid request, stale `registryVersion` or quorum out of range | Fix the request |
| `401` | RequestAuth or session token invalid or expired | Sign again |
| `402` | Payment required or rejected (x402), or the source itself is paywalled | Pay as instructed in `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` |
| `403` | No active subscription, quota exhausted, or no delegate access | Check the subscription |
| `404` | Session routes when sessions are disabled | Use RequestAuth |
| `409` | You already have a round for this source and quorum in this 100 ms tick | Retry after one tick (100 ms plus jitter) |
| `429` | Over the per-consumer rate limit | Wait for `Retry-After` |
| `503` | Gateway at capacity, too few nodes accepted, or round timeout | Wait for `Retry-After` if present, then retry |

`409`, `429` and capacity `503`s are returned before anything is reserved or spent. Any other retry is a new round that spends quota or a payment again.
